# Code of conduct

This project is a small research prototype built for an open hackathon. The standard is simple
and it applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and any discussion around the repository.

## Expected

- Critique the work, not the person. "This claim is unsupported, here is why" is welcome and
  useful; "you clearly don't understand this" is not.
- Assume the other person is acting in good faith until they demonstrate otherwise.
- Respect the project's hard boundary: **this is not a medical device.** Do not push features,
  copy or framing that invite clinical use, and do not present the prototype as clinically
  validated.
- Credit sources. If you bring in data, an image or code, say where it came from and under what
  licence.
- Keep other people's data out of it. Never paste real health records, identifying information or
  credentials into an issue.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing someone's private
information, or dismissing a report of a safety or accessibility defect because it is inconvenient.

## Reporting

Open an issue, or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile if the matter is
sensitive. Reports are handled privately and in good faith. Maintainers may edit, lock or remove
contributions that break this code, and may block repeat offenders.
